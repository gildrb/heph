The **[derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative)** of a function is the rate of change of the function's output relative to its input value.

**Derivative** may also refer to:

## In mathematics and economics

- [Brzozowski derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brzozowski_derivative) in the theory of formal languages
- [Covariant derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covariant_derivative), a way of specifying a derivative along [tangent vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_vector) of a [[Manifold]] with a [connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_(mathematics) "Connection (mathematics)").
- [Exterior derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_derivative), an extension of the concept of the [differential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushforward_(differential) "Pushforward (differential)") of a function to [differential forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_form) of higher degree.
- [Formal derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_derivative), an operation on elements of a polynomial ring which mimics the form of the derivative from calculus
- [Fréchet derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fr%C3%A9chet_derivative), a derivative defined on [normed spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space).
- [Gateaux derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gateaux_derivative), a generalization of the concept of [directional derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directional_derivative) in [differential calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_calculus).
- [Lie derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_derivative), the change of a [tensor field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_field) (including scalar functions, [vector fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_field) and [one-forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-form)), along the [flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_(mathematics) "Flow (mathematics)") defined by another vector field.
- [Radon–Nikodym derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon%E2%80%93Nikodym_derivative) in measure theory
- [Derivative (set theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_(set_theory)), a concept applicable to normal functions
- [Derivative (graph theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_(graph_theory)), an alternative term for a line graph
- [Derivative (finance)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_(finance) "Derivative (finance)"), a contract whose value is derived from that of other quantities
- [Derivative suit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_suit) or derivative action, a type of lawsuit filed by shareholders of a corporation

## In science and engineering

- [Derivative (chemistry)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_(chemistry) "Derivative (chemistry)"), a type of compound which is a product of the process of derivatization
- [Derivative (linguistics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative_(linguistics)), the process of forming a new word on the basis of an existing word, e.g. happiness and unhappy from happy
- [Aeroderivative gas turbine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeroderivative_gas_turbine), a mechanical drive gas turbine derived from an aero engine gas turbine
