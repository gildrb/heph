In [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics), the term "**characteristic function**" may refer to:

- The [indicator function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indicator_function) of a [subset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subset)
- [Characteristic function (probability theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_function_(probability_theory) "Characteristic function (probability theory)")
- The characteristic function of a [cooperative game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperative_game_theory) in [game theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_theory).
- The [characteristic polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_polynomial) in [linear algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_algebra).
- The [characteristic state function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_state_function) in [statistical mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_mechanics).
- The [receiver operating characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Receiver_operating_characteristic) in statistical [decision theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_theory).
