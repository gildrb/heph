**Martingale** may refer to:

- [Martingale (probability theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(probability_theory) "Martingale (probability theory)"), a stochastic process in which the conditional expectation of the next value, given the current and preceding values, is the current value
- [Martingale (tack)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(tack) "Martingale (tack)") for horses
- [Martingale (collar)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(collar) "Martingale (collar)") for dogs and other animals
- [Martingale (betting system)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(betting_system) "Martingale (betting system)"), in 18th century France
- a [dolphin striker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolphin_striker), a spar aboard a sailing ship
- In the sport of [fencing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fencing), a martingale is a strap attached to the sword handle to prevent a sword from being dropped if disarmed
- In the theatrical lighting industry, *martingale* is an obsolete term for a [twofer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twofer), or occasionally a threefer
- [Martingale (clothing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(clothing) "Martingale (clothing)"), a strap controlling the fullness of the clothing material.
